# Pump Price

A [GrumpyTechBro](https://x.com/GrumpyTechBro) joint.

A web calculator for gasoline, diesel, and a few consumer prices. Not a game. No score. You get god levers (countries, refineries, taxes, policies). A US map and a graph move when the levers move.

Prices are supposed to come out of supply and demand for heavy and light, sour and sweet crude, fit to historical series. A hardcoded bump is a bug.
