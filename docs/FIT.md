# The fit

Locked 2026-09-27. The score is already in [PRICE-IDENTITY.md](PRICE-IDENTITY.md). This note is how a miss is allowed to get smaller.

## Fat is a market

Not one market.

Soybean oil trades on the Chicago exchange. The Gulf cargo is priced as a differential to that future, the same shape as a crude that is not the headline barrel. Argus publishes that differential. This is the fat series we can store.

Used cooking oil, tallow, and distillers corn oil are assessments, not that exchange. The Agriculture Department prints tallow and grease in dollars per hundredweight, a few loads at a time. Those can be a gap. They cannot be the headline until a series is in the repo.

The reason a barrel of fat beat a barrel of crude is not the fat price alone. A renewable-fuel credit trades (the D4 credit). California's low-carbon credit trades. The tax credit is a rule, and the rule changed. Argus builds a renewable-diesel price as ordinary diesel plus that stack. Take the stack away and the fat barrel often does not clear.

Biofuel is now about half of United States soybean oil use, in the trade press. The fat that becomes diesel is fat that did not become food, and the same hydrotreater can make jet instead. The fit caps renewable diesel at measured production in the history, and at a feedstock limit when the player moves the lever. It is not an unlimited diesel supply.

## Two layers

Layer one is the harbor to the pump. Historical crude, historical harbor gasoline, historical harbor diesel, the tax, and a lag. This should land close. It tests the path in [PATH.md](PATH.md). It does not test the levers. A success here is not the 1 percent we promised. It is the markup.

Layer two is the levers to the harbor. Capacity on or off, country barrels by quality, demand as the measured percent, and fat plus credits for the renewable-diesel slice. The output is the crack. The harbor prices are the tape, not an input. Handing layer two the harbor price is copying the tape.

## Speculation is a gap, not a name

A later-month future minus today's spot, same barrel. The month will arrive. That part is certain. Whether the story in the later month shows up in the spot is not.

The gap is the market's expectation, plus the cost of waiting. A positive gap means the market pays more for the later month than for the barrel now. A negative gap means it pays more for now. Near the end of a contract the front month and the spot are the same barrel, so the gap that can be large is a later month against today's spot, not the front month on its last day.

After the month arrives we score the future the same way we score the model: the percent by which the spot missed what that future had said. One percent means the expectation showed up. Ten percent means it did not, cleanly. Twenty percent means that bet did not matter at the size the paper claimed. The score is reported. It is not subtracted from the retail miss to make the model look finished.

April 2020 is the warning already on the reel. The front contract went below zero because it could be delivered into full tanks. The later months did not. The gap was real, and it was about storage, and it was not the pump.

## Order

Each step is scored and kept, even when it is ugly.

1. Crude divided by 42, plus the tax, plus a lag. No demand, no plants. Write down the percent.
2. Add measured demand and the plants that were actually on. The percent is product supplied over the baseline. It is not searched.
3. Add measured distillate stocks and measured exports, for diesel only.
4. Add measured renewable diesel, capped, and the public fat price. Credits only if their series is in the repo.
5. Show the curve gap beside the leftover. Do not fit it. If the retail leftover is 20 percent or more after the measured steps, the missing factor is still missing. The gap may explain it. The gap does not get a coefficient.

No step is refit after looking at Midnight Hammer or anything later. The week of June 16, 2025 is the last week inside the fit. One score on the weeks after, then we stop touching the coefficients.

National numbers first. A state is the district plus the survey gap. Fifty state models are not the fit.

Groceries wait until layer two has a score. Each item against diesel and gasoline separately. Paper is allowed to come out near zero.
