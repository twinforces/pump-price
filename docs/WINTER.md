# Winter

Locked 2026-09-27. Winter diesel costs more than gasoline at the pump. That premium is already in the diesel harbor. It is not a separate adder on the sign, and it is not the year-round leftover.

January 2015 through the week of June 16, 2025. March through June 2020 left out. The extra 6 cents of federal diesel tax is taken out of the pump comparison, so the gap is not the tax.

Diesel pump minus gasoline pump, January minus July:

| | January minus July |
| --- | --- |
| Gulf | 22 cents |
| Midwest | 27 cents |
| East Coast | 16 cents |

Gulf diesel over its own harbor, after federal tax and 20 cents of Texas tax, is about 54 cents in January and about 55 cents in July. January minus July on that leftover is about 4 cents. Gasoline's leftover is a few cents higher in summer, not winter.

So a cold month explains why the diesel sign is further ahead of the gasoline sign. It does not explain why the diesel sign sits about 54 cents over the diesel harbor in July the same as in January. Nothing is added on top of the harbor for winter.
