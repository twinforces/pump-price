# A note on the plants

Locked 2026-09-27, from a same-day note. The week he named is after the fit. Nothing here changes a coefficient. The pump formula does not grow a new term.

## What matches

The week ending September 18, 2026, refineries ran at 94.0 percent of operable capacity. That is the weekly status report: 16.8 million barrels a day, down 2.8 points from the week before, so the week before was 96.8 percent. "97.5" is the right neighborhood, not the printed week. The same report has distillate stocks 12 percent under the five-year average. Spare capacity at the plant and a tight product tank can both be true.

That slack is a lever on the harbor, not on the sign. A flood at 97 percent moves the harbor more than the same flood at 94. Harvey, the Texas freeze, and Ida already showed the other half: once the harbor moves, the pump follows, and the week stays inside the band.

The Houston plant that matches the plastic-recycling sentence is LyondellBasell's Ship Channel refinery. It stopped making fuel in early 2025, and the site is being turned into chemical recycling. The closure was on the calendar before 2025. The "nobody would buy it, so they quit" motive is his, and it is not locked.

Fuels sold forward are already in the path. January diesel need not exist yet for someone to have locked the price. For that buyer, replacement cost is the locked price, not this week's harbor. The sign is still the higher of that replacement and what is already in the tank.

A loss of refining capacity outside the country, Russian plants included, is a plant lever on the harbor. It is not a separate adder on the US pump.

## What is not locked

The Illinois plant in the news this month is ExxonMobil's Joliet. It lost power on September 13 and then took on floodwater. A week later the restart was still open. "It is back up" is not confirmed here. BP's Whiting plant is in Indiana, not Illinois.

Cutting off all exports, and fruit prices rising a few months later, is not a series. Exports stay a measured diesel lever. Fruit is not in the basket. The handful of months is not a clock we have counted.
