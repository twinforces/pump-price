# The later month did not see the move

Locked 2026-09-28. The futures bet, graded after the month arrived. Not fed back into the pump. The file ends April 5, 2024.

Daily New York Mercantile Exchange closes for Cushing crude. Contract 1 is the front month. Contract 2 is about one month further. Contract 4 is about three months further. The grade pairs a day's later contract with the front month on the first trading day on or after that many calendar months. A match more than 10 days late is dropped. Spring 2020 is left out of the count and left on the chart. About 2,200 days in each grade, from 2015 through the arrivals the file can still see.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One month | $4.30 | $4.18 | 52 percent of days | 69 percent | 8 percent |
| Three months | $6.70 | $6.27 | 50 percent of days | 47 percent | 4 percent |

The predicted change and the change that happened agree only weakly, about 0.2. Half the days is a coin. The miss is most of the wander. Traders were not early.

The curve itself was flat. The later contract sat about 20 cents above the front month. That is the storage dime, not a view that the price should be higher. When the month arrived, the barrel was a little above the quote, not below it: about 60 cents at one month, about $1.50 at three months, in the middle of the days. The market was not leaning rich. It was not leaning. The jumps came from somewhere the curve did not show.
