# The delays

Locked 2026-09-27. They are not one clock, and they are not all in the score.

The harbor in the score is already finished gasoline and diesel on the Gulf. The refinery is behind that price, not between it and the pump.

| Step | How long | In the score? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A ship of crude, Persian Gulf to the US Gulf the long way | about 40 to 45 days | No. The futures price moves the day the news hits. The barrels do not. |
| Crude already at the refinery, turned into fuel | about 3 days, once it is on site | No. |
| Finished fuel, Gulf Coast to New York on the Colonial pipeline | gasoline about 15 days, diesel about 19 days, at about 5 miles an hour | No. The Gulf pump does not ride that pipe. The East Coast does. |
| The pile at the refinery and the terminal | about 25 days of gasoline sales, about 33 days of diesel | Yes. That is each fuel's own cover. |
| The station's own tank | several days at a corner station, about a day at a busy truck stop | Researched. Not added on top of the pile. |

The score's delay, for a Gulf pump, is the pile. The voyage, the three days inside the refinery, and the two weeks on the pipe are written down and not yet run.
