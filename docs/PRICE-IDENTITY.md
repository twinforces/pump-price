# Price identity

Locked 2026-09-27. This is the note the model is allowed to code against. A body that is not in this file does not ship.

## What a price is

Retail gasoline or diesel, dollars per gallon, taxes in, for one week.

The crack is the harbor product minus crude, in dollars per gallon. Tax is added after the crack. Tax does not move the crack. The federal gasoline tax is 18.4 cents and it did not change in any chapter we study.

A state price is the fuel-district price plus that state's survey gap. The gap is data. It is not a coefficient. Two states with different gaps do not get the same price. Alaska and Hawaii diesel are not the West Coast federal diesel number. California diesel is that federal print. The other diesel states are a station survey.

One dollar a barrel is 2.4 cents a gallon because a barrel is 42 gallons. That is arithmetic. It is not the claim that the whole dollar arrives at the pump in the same week.

## Demand

Demand is a percent of a measured baseline. 100 percent is that baseline. 80 percent is four fifths of it. Zero is none.

In the test against history, the percent is measured product supplied divided by the baseline. It is not chosen so the price will land. The player may move the percent. That is the lever. Fitting the percent to the price is cheating, same as copying the tape.

## Plants

Off removes that plant's crude barrels and the gasoline those barrels would have made.

A plant converted to renewable diesel is not off. Crude in is zero. Gasoline out is zero. Renewable diesel barrels are a separate supply, from fat, and they count at the diesel pump only. They are not in the crude pie.

Two plants off are two plants off. The price effect of both together is not required to equal the sum of each alone. That test waits until a price effect exists. The capacity test does not wait: the barrels add.

## The fit window

Midnight Hammer is the United States strike on Iranian nuclear sites on June 22, 2025. It is not the February 28, 2026 closure of the Strait of Hormuz. Those are different events. The second one is later, and it stays unseen for the same reason.

The fit may use weekly prices through the last survey week before that strike, the week of June 16, 2025. The week of the strike, and every week after, including the strait, is not an input to the fit. It is the grade.

## The score

The miss is a percent of the recorded price, absolute, so a high guess and a low guess of the same size are the same miss.

| Miss | What we call it |
| --- | --- |
| 1 percent or less | The success. |
| 10 percent | Not a success. Also not yet proof that a factor is missing. |
| 20 percent or more | A factor is missing. It may be one we can measure and left out. It may be one we cannot measure. Speculation is allowed as that name. |

Speculation is not a slider. Naming it does not shrink the miss. A coefficient dialed until the miss falls under 1 percent is the tape copied by another name.

There is no cents tolerance. A 4 dollar gallon and a 2 dollar gallon are judged on the same percent.
