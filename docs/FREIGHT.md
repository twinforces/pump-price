# The truck burns diesel

Locked 2026-09-27. The delivery cost of the fuel moves with the diesel price, because the truck that brings it burns diesel. Often the same truck brings both. A Texas delivery-fee schedule bills the gasoline and the diesel on one load as one delivery.

The piece that fluctuates is the fuel the truck burns. A worked example, not a fit: 100 miles, 6 miles to the gallon, 8,500 gallons on the truck. A one-dollar move in diesel is about a fifth of a cent on the gallon delivered. From a two-dollar diesel to a five-dollar diesel, the adder goes from about four-tenths of a cent to about a cent.

That sits inside the leftover. It is not why the leftover is 22 cents for gasoline or 54 for diesel, and it is not a 10 percent miss. The driver and the truck are a larger freight, and that part does not have to move with diesel. There is no measured Gulf station freight rate here, so none is locked.

The pipeline tariff is a filed number. It is not this truck.
