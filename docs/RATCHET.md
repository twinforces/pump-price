# The ratchet, run

Locked 2026-09-27. The sign is the higher of this week's harbor and the average cost of the gallons still in the tank, plus the tax, plus the leftover. The leftover is the median gap on the weeks outside spring 2020. Spring does not set it.

Gasoline and diesel do not share a tank. The customers are different.

## Two clocks

The first clock was a station tank. A normal week replaces half of it, so the cover is two weeks. When sales fall, the same gallons last longer. It is not allowed to exceed eight weeks. That length was not searched.

The second clock is the bulk tank, the stocks at refineries and terminals, divided by that fuel's own sales. In 2019 the median was 25 days for gasoline and 33 days for diesel. Diesel sits about a week longer. Those are the published stocks, not a pair of lengths picked for a better score.

A long stock is a volume. It is not proof the barrels still in it were bought at the old price. If the tank kept taking the cheap harbor, the average cost falls even while the days of cover stay high.

March 30, 2020 goes from 42 percent low to 28. May 4 stays about 22 percent low. The Omicron week moves inside. October 31, 2022 does not. January 2015 diesel stays outside.

## Each fuel's own cover

The window is that week's stocks divided by that week's sales, in weeks, and at least one week.

| | Gasoline | Diesel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A normal year | 25 days | 33 days |
| Half the weeks within | 1.9 percent | 3.1 percent |
| Outside 10 percent | 7 weeks | 15 weeks |
| March 30, 2020 | 12 percent low, on 37 days of cover | 11 percent low, on 31 days |
| May 4, 2020 | 23 percent low, on 38 days | 16 percent low, on 48 days |
| December 6, 2021 | inside, 8 percent low | inside, 5 percent low |
| January 12, 2015 | inside | 15 percent low, on 35 days |

March 30 is almost inside once gasoline is allowed its own longer cover. May is not. By then a tank that had kept buying the cheap harbor would already be cheap, and the sign was not. Diesel's extra week of cover is real, and it is not why January 2015 or the week of the invasion spike stay outside. In March 2022 diesel cover was only 25 days, shorter than normal, and the sign was still 14 percent high.

The ordinary week moves a little. The tail is the story, and the tail is not finished.
