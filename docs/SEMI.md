# The Tesla Semi

Locked 2026-09-27. Revised the same day: not worth a lever.

The arithmetic stands. A truck at about 7 miles a gallon and 100,000 miles a year burns about one barrel a day. Distillate is 3.9 million barrels a day. Dozens of trucks, or even a year of the Sparks factory if it ever builds 50,000 and each one replaces a diesel mile, do not belong in the model.

There is no slider. There is no term in the fit. If a future census shows the fleet is large enough to see in the federal diesel survey, this note gets reopened. Until then it is a receipt, not a control.
