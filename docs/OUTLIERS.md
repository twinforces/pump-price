# The weeks outside the band

Locked 2026-09-27. Gulf Coast, January 2015 through June 16, 2025. Outside means more than 10 percent off the pump, after the harbor, the federal tax, Texas's 20 cents, and the leftover held still.

A storm that shuts refineries is not what these weeks are. The harbor price already contains that. The pump followed, and the week stayed inside.

| Storm | What the gasoline score did |
| --- | --- |
| Harvey, late August 2017 | Inside 6 percent |
| The Texas freeze, February 2021 | Inside 2 percent |
| Ida, late August 2021 | Inside 5 percent |

The outsiders are weeks when the harbor moved and the sign did not.

## Gasoline, 10 weeks

Eight are March 16 through May 4, 2020. Sales stopped. The tank did not turn. The worst is March 30, the pump at $1.76 while the harbor was 42 cents.

December 6, 2021. Omicron. Crude fell about $13 a barrel in the week after Thanksgiving. The Gulf harbor fell from $2.26 to $2.01. The Gulf pump fell one penny, $3.00 to $2.99. The agency wrote that week up as a crude forecast cut, and the diesel drop was the largest weekly retail drop since March 2020. The sign had barely started.

October 31, 2022. The other direction, and the only gasoline week on this list where the guess was too high. The harbor jumped 26 cents for one Friday, in a week when diesel futures were the story and gasoline futures rose with them. The Gulf pump fell 4 cents. No storm.

## Diesel, 26 weeks

Three weeks in January 2015. The oil-price collapse. The sign was still high.

Twelve weeks from March 16 through June 1, 2020. Same crash as gasoline, and diesel's sign fell even slower. The worst is May 4.

December 6, 2021. The same Omicron week. Just outside the band.

2022, thirteen weeks if you count May 2023's two barely-outside weeks separately. March 21 is the week after the invasion spike: gasoline stayed inside, diesel's sign was still $4.96 while the Gulf harbor had fallen to $3.28. November 21 through December 12 is four weeks while distillate stocks were the shortage, the East Coast barrel was far above the Gulf, and Europe was rearranging diesel. May 1 and May 8, 2023 are 11 and 10 percent low. No named storm in any of them.

One diesel week, May 2, 2022, is the other direction. The harbor got ahead of the sign.
