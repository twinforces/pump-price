# First score

Locked 2026-09-27. Step one of the fit, and it is ugly on purpose. No demand, no plants, no strait. Nothing after June 16, 2025 was used.

## The guess

Same week. The Monday pump price, against the Friday Cushing crude price three days earlier.

Estimated pump = crude dollars per barrel, divided by 42, plus the federal tax. Gasoline tax is 18.4 cents. Diesel tax is 24.4 cents. No state tax. No refinery margin. No searched lag.

The chart is the signed percent: estimated minus actual, divided by the actual pump price. Below zero means the guess is cheaper than the pump.

Every week is past the line where a factor is missing. The missing factor is not a mystery. This guess leaves out the state tax, the crack, and the path from the harbor to the station. April 2020 is the spike because crude collapsed and the pump did not follow it to 26 cents.

Later steps have to beat a guess that sits about 42 percent below gasoline and about 47 percent below diesel. They do not get to claim the 1 percent until they do.
