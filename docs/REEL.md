# The reel

Locked 2026-09-27. The first thing the calculator plays is recorded prices, animated. Not a story about presidents. The administration names are the chapter titles that were asked for. The shocks inside them are crude, demand, and a closed strait.

Hormuz is the last chapter. It is not in the first reel.

## Chapters

| Order | Chapter | Window | What we are looking at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | COVID | March 2020 through the end of 2020 | Demand collapsed. On April 20, 2020 the front-month light sweet contract at Cushing settled below zero, because storage was full and that contract can be delivered. |
| 2 | 2021 through 2024 | January 20, 2021 through January 20, 2025 | The fuel event in this window is the 2022 spike, after Russia invaded Ukraine in February 2022. The chapter title is the calendar that was asked for. The invasion is not a party. |
| 3 | 2025 up to the strait | January 20, 2025 through February 27, 2026 | The baseline before the shock. |
| 4 | Hormuz, later | February 28, 2026 onward | An Iran war starts in the public record on February 28, 2026, and the Strait of Hormuz is shut. We do not open this chapter until the first three have been watched. |

## What "animated" means

A week at a time. The national gasoline and diesel lines draw themselves. The map takes the weekly federal print for each fuel district, and the nine states where gasoline is actually surveyed. The graph is the same numbers, not a second opinion.

This playback does not wait on the price identity. It is the tape. When a model exists, its homework is to replay these three chapters and show the miss in public. Matching the tape by being fed the tape is not a pass.

The February 2026 shock is already inside the gasoline series. Holding it back is a choice about the order of the movie, not a missing file.

A magazine chart is not the tape. The Economist in May 2026 described United States retail gasoline rising from just over 3 dollars in February toward about 4 dollars and 60 cents. We draw the federal weekly print instead.

## Still not the model

The reel shows what the prices did. It does not say a refinery, a tax, or a closed strait produced them until the identity is written and misses on purpose on chapters 1 through 3. Hormuz is the chapter we do not peek at while fitting.
