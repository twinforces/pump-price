# Gold does not calm the barrel

Locked 2026-09-27. Pricing crude in gold does not remove the wander. No gold ratio is fitted.

World Bank monthly prices, nominal dollars. West Texas Intermediate, the London gold fix in the same sheet, copper, and silver. January 2015 through June 2025 unless said otherwise.

The month-to-month gold link is zero. Once the shared rise of the decade is out, gold and crude move against each other. That is the opposite of one dollar story.

A barrel priced in gold wandered about as much as a barrel priced in dollars. Leaving out spring 2020, the dollar price varied by about 26 percent of its average, and the gold price of a barrel by about 24 percent. A barrel cost as little as about 0.02 ounces and as much as about 0.06. April 2020 was about 0.01 ounce. June 2022 was about 0.06. By January 2026 gold had run to about $4,750 and a barrel cost about 0.013 ounce. Gold got expensive. Crude did not keep up.

Copper moves with crude more than gold does. It is an industrial metal. It is not a currency, and a month-to-month link of about 0.3 does not pin the price.

Copper is calmer than crude inside a month, and not by being a metal that sits still for a decade. Leaving out spring 2020, a typical month moves copper about 3 percent and crude about 6 percent. Gold's typical month is about 2 percent. Gold jumps less and trends more: across the decade gold wandered about 30 percent of its average, copper about 23 percent, crude about 26 percent. Copper's smaller month does not make the barrel calm. Crude still jumps about twice as far.
