# Second score

Locked 2026-09-27. Three guesses, same 512 weeks, January 2015 through June 16, 2025. The Monday pump. The Friday price three days earlier. Nothing after June 16, 2025.

## What was added

Taxes are the Energy Information Administration's monthly national average, federal and state together, from the pump-component history. A week uses the tax of its month.

The harbor price is the Gulf Coast spot. Conventional regular gasoline. Ultra-low-sulfur diesel. That price already contains the crack. Using it tests the path from the harbor to the pump. It does not test a refinery, a country, or demand. A lever model is not allowed to be handed this price.

The markup is one number for the whole decade: the median of pump minus harbor minus tax. Gasoline 43.9 cents. Diesel 67.1 cents. It was read off the leftover. It was not moved week to week. Because it is the median leftover, the center of that line sits near zero on purpose. The test is whether the leftover stays still.

## The percent

Estimated minus the pump, as a percent of the pump.

| Guess | Gasoline, typical | Diesel, typical | Inside 10 percent | Inside 1 percent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crude + federal tax | 42 percent low | 47 percent low | None | None |
| Crude + federal and state tax | 31 percent low | 36 to 38 percent low | None | None |
| Harbor + taxes, no markup | 16 percent low | 21 percent low | 5 percent of gasoline weeks, almost no diesel weeks | None |
| Harbor + taxes + the one markup | About zero at the median | About zero at the median | 98 percent of gasoline weeks, 91 percent of diesel weeks | 16 percent of gasoline weeks, 12 percent of diesel weeks |

Ten percent is not the success. One percent is. The one markup does not earn it. Gasoline's worst week is March 30, 2020, 33 percent low. Diesel's is May 4, 2020, 22 percent low. The pump did not fall as fast as the harbor.

Demand and plants are not in this score. A cents-per-percent for them has not been earned, and it will not be searched until the harbor price is something the model produces instead of receives.
