# The harbor over crude

Locked 2026-09-27. The path from the harbor to the sign can sit on one constant per fuel. The harbor over crude cannot.

Crude divided by 42 is the gallon of oil. The Gulf harbor minus that gallon, January 2015 through the week of June 13, 2025. The two harbors and the crude quote are the same Friday.

| | Typical gap | Low | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gasoline | 39 cents | about 5 cents | $1.39 |
| Diesel | 48 cents | about 10 cents | $2.30 |

One constant, spring 2020 set aside, 528 weeks:

| | Typical miss | Inside 1 percent | Outside 10 percent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gasoline | 6 percent | 53 weeks | 144 |
| Diesel | 7 percent | 59 weeks | 182 |

That fails the bar the pump passed. The outside weeks are spread across the decade. Diesel's are heavy in 2016, 2020, 2022, and 2023. Not one winter.

The season is real and smaller than those swings. Gasoline's gap is about 21 cents fatter in May than in January. Diesel's is about 17 cents fatter in February than in July. That is the driving season and the furnace, already visible in the harbor. A month-shaped constant would not eat a $2 diesel gap.

Late April 2022, the diesel harbor was about $2.30 over crude. The constant was 48 cents.

No constant is subtracted from the harbor. The crack is the harbor minus crude. It is not a fitted knob.
