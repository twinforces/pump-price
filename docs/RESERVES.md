# Reserves, and a market that does not know its type

Locked 2026-09-28. A note on the trading experiment and on the spring 2026 tanks. Not a fitted bubble.

A market that does not know whether it is a shortage will trade as if it is, until someone can check. That is the strait, not an ordinary month. In an ordinary month the later contract sat about 20 cents over the front month and then missed the move. Nobody was leaning. The curve was not an "A" market that everyone had agreed on.

The check is downstream, because the strait itself is fog. The US tanks in 2026 do not tell one story.

| | January | April | June |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| US crude | $60 | $100 | $87 |
| Commercial crude stocks | 422 million barrels | 464 | 415 |
| Strategic reserve | 414 million barrels | 407 | 336 |
| Distillate stocks | 129 million barrels | 110 | 105 |

From January to April the crude price rose about $40 while commercial crude stocks rose about 40 million barrels. The US crude tank was filling. It was not the shortage. The government reserve barely moved until May and June, after the price was already up, and then it fell by about 70 million barrels. Distillate stocks did draw, about 20 million barrels by April. That matches the diesel harbor pulling away from crude. It does not tell us how many barrels were still coming through the strait.

Everyone having a reserve is why a hole in the flow does not have to be a hole in the price in the same week. It is also why a price can run before any tank the public can see has emptied. Spring 2026 did the second one for crude.
