# Markets

Locked 2026-09-27. If a fuel is traded, we use the trade. We do not add a term for "prices are artificially high." A leftover after the fit is a miss, and the miss stays in the receipts. It is not a villain.

## The barrel on the news

West Texas Intermediate and Brent are real contracts. They are both light sweet. Quoting one of them as "the price of oil" is the fiction. The other three qualities are different barrels, and they do not move as one price.

PIMCO is a bond manager. The barrel people are Platts, now S&P Global Commodity Insights, and Argus. They publish a price and an assay for a named stream: a field, or a blend of fields. Not a country, and not a mood.

Examples from the Platts Americas crude guide (July 2026), gravity in API degrees, sulfur in percent:

| Stream | Gravity | Sulfur | Rough seat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| West Texas Intermediate, Cushing | 38 to 42 | at most 0.42 | Light sweet |
| Mars | 29.4 | 1.87 | Heavier and sour |
| Western Canadian Select | 21.8 | 3.63 | Heavy sour |
| Light Louisiana Sweet | 38.5 | 0.40 | Light sweet |

A country lever is a stack of those streams. Saudi Arabia is not one slice. It sells extra light through heavy. Turning the country off removes each of its streams from whichever slice that stream sits in.

## What is actually traded in public

| Contract | What it is | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CME crude, ticker CL | Light sweet, Cushing, dollars per barrel. 1,000 barrels a contract. | The world. |
| Brent, ICE | Light sweet North Sea, seaborne. | The world. |
| CME gasoline, ticker RB | Reformulated blendstock for oxygenate blending, New York Harbor, dollars per gallon. 42,000 gallons. Ethanol is added later, at the terminal, because ethanol cannot ride the pipeline. | The price on a pump, and not California. |
| CME diesel, ticker HO | New York Harbor ultra-low-sulfur diesel, dollars per gallon. The benchmark for diesel, heating oil, and jet. | The national pump price. Heating oil and diesel share this contract and then diverge. |

The street price is this bulk price, plus the district, plus state tax, plus the spec, plus the station. Showing the harbor contract as the pump price is the same cheat as showing one crude as the world.

Heavy sour has a public discount: Western Canadian Select against the light sweet contract. Light sour and heavy sweet are mostly Platts or Argus assessments of named streams. Those books are licensed. Until we have a receipt for a series we can store, those two prices are "assessment, not in the repo," not a made-up quote.

## The pie

The screen gets a pie of the world oil mix in four slices: light sweet, light sour, heavy sweet, heavy sour. A second pie is what United States refineries actually ran, by fuel district, because the country exports light barrels and imports heavy ones. The world mix and the still mix are not the same picture.

The federal "Crude Oil Input Qualities" table is sulfur and gravity of what the stills charged, monthly, back to 1985, by district. June 2026, United States average sulfur was 1.35 percent. That is an average, not a pie. https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_pnp_crq_a_EPC0_YCS_pct_m.htm

World volumes by gravity and sulfur are published by Eni in the World Energy Review, through 2022 in the edition we opened. Their bins are finer than four. They have medium, and medium sour. Our four slices are a rollup. The cut points (what gravity is "light," what sulfur is "sweet," and where medium goes) get written down before a slice is drawn. They are not implied.

An old Bureau of Mines assay file runs only through 1983. It is not the live pie.

## Groceries

Diesel moving hamburger, bread, eggs, milk, or paper is a fit. Each item against its own history, with diesel and gasoline as separate prices, because those two prices are not the same series. If paper's diesel term comes out near zero, it stays near zero. Eggs are allowed a shock that is not fuel. No coefficient ships because a chat said 0.15 cents.

A wedge for manipulation, politics, or a claim that the posted price is fake. Taxes and specs are already levers, and they are real. They are not a conspiracy.
