# Velocity is the wrong derivative of this series

Locked 2026-09-28. A note on an overnight idea, not a fitted model.

The textbook cross fails for oil and for electricity in opposite ways. Electricity in California around 2000 could not be stored, households did not see the wholesale price, and the last plant was a cliff. The market cleared by a blackout. Oil can be stored, and wells turn on and off, so quantity moves first and the price is what is left. That is why an ordinary month shows supply sitting on demand while the price wanders. The curves are not too old. They are the wrong picture of a good that waits in a tank.

Adaptive wells are real and unmeasured here. A well that stays shut until the price clears its cost is a step, with a lag, not a straight supply line. We have not counted those steps.

The rate of change of published world demand does not price the barrel. January 2015 through December 2025, spring 2020 left out, the strait left out:

| | Crude price | Change in the crude price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change in demand | +0.04 | −0.03 |
| Change in that change | +0.01 | −0.01 |
| Change in supply |  | +0.13 |
| Change in the gap |  | +0.12 |

A typical month moved demand by about 1 million barrels a day and the price by about $3. Those did not travel together. The monthly demand number is revised and smooth. It is not the surprise a trader was wrong about. That surprise is the futures test still waiting: what the later month cost, against what the barrel cost when the month arrived.
